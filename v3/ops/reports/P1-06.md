# P1-06 report: Router::hedged with a latency budget

Status: done

What I built:
- `Router::new` / `ranked(now)` / `hedged(req, budget, out, cancel)` in `alexia-core/src/router.rs`.
- Ranking: drops providers with no minute or day headroom (`request_count` against `rpm`/`rpd`),
  then sorts by v2 `sunk`, then median first byte of the last 10 ok tries (fast, then untried,
  then slower than budget), then routers last, then larger `size_b`, then (provider, model).
- Hedging: each attempt is `tokio::spawn`ed with a child token and its own chunk channel, and a
  watcher in a `JoinSet` reports its first chunk or its end. Two start, one more each budget tick,
  a failure starts the next. At most 4 running and 6 started. Never two from one provider at once.
- Winner: losers are cancelled and recorded `lost-race`, the winner's chunks go to `out`, then
  `ok` try + `record_usage` (`writing_ms` = done minus first byte). Every start is `record_request`ed.
- No candidates (only paid models in the catalog) gives `Other("No free model can answer right now.")`.

Problems or doubts:
- Tests use real time, not `tokio::time::pause()`. alexia-core's tokio has no `test-util` feature
  and Cargo.toml was not on my touch list. The races are built so they cannot flip: the loser
  waits 60 s, lower bounds are exact, upper bounds are 20 s. In the first test the slow side
  answers at 60 s instead of the spec's 800 ms, for that reason.
- Busy tries halve in 2 minutes (v2 `BUSY_HALF_LIFE`, health.ts) and every other failure in 1 hour.
  The spec only names the hour, but v2's `sunk` does both. Without it, one 429 would sink a model
  for an hour.
- Outcome mapping: Busy is `busy`. NeedsKey and Refused are `refused`. Unreachable, Other and a
  provider's own Cancelled are `unreachable`. `timeout` is never written, because openai.rs turns
  first-byte timeouts into Busy. `status` is always 0, since ProviderError carries no HTTP status.
- Attempts stopped by the caller's `cancel` count in `record_request` but get no `tries` row.
  None of v2's outcome strings fits, and a row would wrongly sink the model.
- If the winner fails after sending text, its error is returned and nothing else takes over,
  because text was already forwarded.
- `ranked(now)` ranks against `Budget::CHAT`. `hedged` uses the budget it is given.
