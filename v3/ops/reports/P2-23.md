What I built:
- `McpHost::reload(id)`: claims `Reloading` under the spawn lock, waits for in-flight calls (bounded by `call_ms`), stops with `StopReason::Disabled`, swaps in the re-parsed manifest, re-creates tables, emits `Loaded` + `ToolsChanged`. Resident plugins respawn at once, lazy ones on the next call.
- New calls during `Reloading` wait in `ensure_running` (bounded by `call_ms`).
- Bad manifest: old process kept, `PluginStatus.sentence` = reason, `reload` returns `Err(Refused)`, no `Refused` event.
- Change detection (`supervise::Stamp`): mtime+size of plugin.json and in-folder entry files, plus the `version` field. Checked each watcher heartbeat and before each call / tool listing (so a stopped lazy plugin also picks up changes). No new dependency.
