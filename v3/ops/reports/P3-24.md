What I built:
- `mail.send` (tool `mail_send`) in google-account and microsoft-account: `readOnlyHint: false, destructiveHint: true, openWorldHint: true`. With no `approved_draft` it refuses with "Sending needs your yes on the draft first." The token is checked in the plugin (its shape, issued in the last 60 s, not spent before) and spent. Then the plugin asks the mail desk back through a new capability, `mail.approval`, whether this is the owner's yes for this exact to/subject/text/replyTo. Only then does it send: Gmail `messages/send` (raw RFC 5322, threaded by In-Reply-To/References), Graph `sendMail` or `/reply`. Never Drafts.
- Scopes: Connect asks for `gmail.send` / `Mail.Send` (passed in index.js; oauth.js untouched). A token answer without the scope, or a 403 on a send, sets the status to "connect once more".
- Sent sample: `mail.list` with `in:sent` may list up to 200 (Graph: the SentItems folder). `mail.read {id, headers: true}` also returns from/to/subject/threadId/reply_to.
- mail-desk `voice.js`: style card from one sampling call over at most 30 trimmed sent messages (quoted text cut). It is kept in kv `voice_card` and rebuilt after a week; no message is stored. `draft_reply` writes `<own_dir>/drafts/<id>.txt` and a `drafts` row. `send_draft` asks through form elicitation; only accept with `send: true` gets a one-time token (60 s) and a `mail.send` call. A no, no answer, a throw or no way to ask keeps the draft. Tool `mail_approval` provides `mail.approval`.
