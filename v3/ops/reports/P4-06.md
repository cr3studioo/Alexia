- Frame rules: one line per message, a "\n" after 5 s of writer silence, empty lines dropped before rx, a line over 16 MiB (or not UTF-8) closes the link with a warn and no content. `closed` fires when either body ends or errors, when `tx` is dropped, or when cancelled.
- ops.rs: `HubOp`, `serve_ops`, `send_op` (5 s → Unreachable). Bodies ≤ 64 KiB (413 above), face.text ≤ 4096 chars, a malformed body → 400 and no callback. `from` comes from connect's peer header, never from the body.
