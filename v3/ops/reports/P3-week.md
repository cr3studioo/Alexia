Feedback, three lines:
1. Under what she brings up, press **Thanks**, **Not useful**, **Later**, **Why?** or **Done**.
2. Too chatty: `/quiet 2h` pauses, `/quiet 00:00 08:00` sets quiet hours, `/quiet off` ends them; `/mute <source>` stops one source (`/mute` lists them). Careful: all plugin notices share the source `notice.items`, so muting it, or replying "stop" to one of them, silences them all (undo on the PC: `alexia-hub mute list`, then `alexia-hub mute remove <number>`).
3. What she may do without asking: `/silent` shows it, `/silent allow <tool id>` adds, `/silent remove <tool id>` takes away.
