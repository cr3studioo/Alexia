What I built:
- `permissions::is_local_only(store)`: kv `_core`/`local_only` == `true`; absent, non-bool or unreadable → false.

  `hedged`, `step`) returns nothing in local-only mode. `hedged` then races only models of
  providers registered `set_provider(.., true)`; `step` treats every job as a local job, skips
  cloud/subscription pins with a note, and never adds the free list. Empty → `NO_LOCAL_MODEL`.
- Hedging off in local-only: `race`/`run_race` take `hedge: bool`; off = one attempt at a time,
  no timer starts, the next candidate only after a failure.
- Small refactor: `models_of(id)` (runtime provider → candidates) shared by `step` and the new
  `local_candidates()`.
