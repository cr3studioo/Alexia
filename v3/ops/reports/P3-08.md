- Must fix 1: pause and quiet-hours holds are no longer written into `not_before`. Dispatch only marks the row `held`. When `not_before` equals that value, the decider ignores it (`decider_view`) and works out the pause and quiet rules again. A Later from the owner sets another value and is kept.

- A released job reply keeps its job: `Say.job = Some(id)`, read from the key (`job_of`).
