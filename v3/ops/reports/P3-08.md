What I built:
- `decide.rs`: `radar`, `decide`, `DecideCtx`, `Decision`. They apply the INTERFACES §2.5 rules in order and are pure functions (re-exported from the crate root).
- `notices.rs`: the notice loop. Intake runs at start and every `collect_every_ms`. It files items once, re-applies the radar to live deadlines, sets a said notice back to `new` when it rises to High or Urgent, and marks done a new or held item that went missing for 3 answered rounds. Dispatch runs after each intake, on Feedback, Active and `_core` SettingsChanged, when a hold ends or an urgent repeat is due, and when a job reply was held. More than 3 SayNow in one pass go out as one Say. Feedback and Active come in over the bus; Lagged re-reads the store. last_active is kept in kv `_core`/`last_active`.
- `lib.rs`: `run` starts the notice loop under the same cancel token and waits for it on shutdown.
- CEO #20: a held job reply is now one write. The row is filed with `not_before`, which decide rule 2 holds on its own; the old `notice_set` that could fail after the reply was said is gone. Only a failed upsert says the reply now. The notice key keeps the job's chat (`job_key`/`job_to`), so the release goes back with `to: Some(origin)`. Job replies are never merged into a burst.
- Added beyond the spec: notices the owner dropped (NotUseful, Mute or a mute) or marked Done are not filed again while the source keeps offering them. Without this, the next round would refile them as `new` and say them again. Kept in memory, and seeded at start from the last 30 days of dropped rows.

Problems or doubts:
- Holds are stored in `not_before`, as the spec says. So a notice held for quiet hours or a pause stays held until then, even if it later turns Urgent or the owner lifts the pause early. A fix needs a way to tell the decider's holds apart from the owner's Later.
- The spec says a source that returned nothing this round counts as not answered. So when a source's last item goes away, nothing closes until that source returns something else.
- decide's quiet-end time is worked out from the local (hour, minute). On a DST night it can be an hour off: an early wake is simply held again, a late one says it an hour late.
