What I built:
- Root cause: idle_lazy_plugin_stops read the pid with host.pid().unwrap() after the first call. IDLE_MS is 1 s, so a stall under load let the idle stop fire first and the unwrap panicked.
- The pid now comes from the recorded Started event, which cannot be missed.
- The wait for the idle stop is widened from 30 s to 60 s, the same style as the P0-12 crash window.
- The final "restarted with a new pid" check now asserts two Started events. A pid read could race or be reused.
- The test still proves a lazy plugin stops after idle: it waits for the pid to clear, requires a Stopped(Idle) event, requires the process gone, and requires zero crashes.
- busy_lazy_plugin_does_not_idle_stop and resident_does_not_idle_stop do not read pid after a call, so they needed no change.
