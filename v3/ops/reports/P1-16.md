Problems or doubts:
- `tokio::time::pause` needs tokio's `test-util` feature, which no Cargo.toml enables, and I may only touch watchdog.rs. Enabling test-util in alexia-hub dev-dependencies would let it use pause.
- No time-zone crate exists. Local time uses the current UTC offset from PowerShell (Windows, run once at boot), UTC elsewhere. A gap across a DST change shows one hour off.
