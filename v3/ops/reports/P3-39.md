What I built:

- `schedule.add` with kind `watch`: `trigger_of` removes `args.unlocked`, then `ScheduleTools::add` (now async) calls `watchable` before `Scheduler::add`. On Err the model gets an error outcome with that sentence and no job row is stored.

- `HostSources::watchable`: every enabled provider of `cap` (refused or unhealthy ones too) must list a tool for `cap`. Every such tool must be reviewed, have `readOnlyHint: true` and not have `destructiveHint: true`. It fails closed on no provider ("Nothing installed can be watched for {cap}."), on a provider with no listed tool, and on a tool list that did not arrive within `per_call`.
