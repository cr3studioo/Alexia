What I built:
- Cause (reproduced with a Character Map the probe started and closed by pid): once focused,
  Character Map shows a tooltip over its grid. The tooltip's drop shadow (class `SysShadow`,
  164x25 pane, WS_EX_TOOLWINDOW, ex 0x800A0) is a top-level window of Character Map's own
  process, in front of the dialog. `elements` started at `FindFirst(Children, ProcessId = pid)`,
  which is the frontmost one, and so walked the empty shadow. `MainWindowHandle` also switched to
  it. It depends on where the pointer rests, hence the flakiness. Not timing, not `warm()`.
- windows.js: `candidatesScript` lists the pid's top-level UIA elements. Pure `pickWindow`
  chooses the frontmost `Window`, else the largest sized one that is not a ToolTip, Menu,
  MenuBar or SysShadow. `elements`, `readElement` and `invoke` start at `FromHandle(<chosen>)`,
  or answer empty when nothing qualifies.
- focus: C# `WindowOf(pid)` (frontmost visible, unowned, non-tool window) replaces
  `MainWindowHandle`. The UWP `FrameOf` fallback is unchanged.
- Cost: one extra PowerShell spawn (no Add-Type, ~200 ms) per pid-targeted elements/read/invoke.
