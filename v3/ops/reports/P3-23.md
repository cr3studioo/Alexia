What I built:
- `parcels.js`: tracking numbers for Zásilkovna, PPL, DPD, GLS, Česká pošta, DHL, UPS, FedEx, Amazon (shape-only numbers match alone; plain-digit ones need the carrier's name and a tracking word); state and eta in English and Czech; a parcel never goes backwards.
- `bills.js`: pre-filter, prompt, and `parseBill` that accepts a model answer only if the due day is a real calendar date, the amount a positive number and the currency 3 letters.

- `desk.js` (poll loop, notices, brief sections, tool data) and `index.js` (wiring, timer from the `every` setting, tools `inbox`, `parcels`, `bills`, `bill_paid`, plus `notice_items`, `notice_done`, `brief_section`). Only `mail.list` and `mail.read` are ever called.
- `plugin.json` (requires, settings `important`/`every`/`status`, tables `seen`/`parcels`/`bills`) and `README.md`.
