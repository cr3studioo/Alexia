Problems or doubts:
- No `tokio::time::pause`: the crate has no `tokio/test-util` and Cargo.toml is off-limits. The
  tests use real time. Waits are capped at 30 s and timing checks are lower bounds, except the
  limits the spec sets itself (ack within 10 ms, at most 5 drafts per second, shutdown under 4 s).
- The lane is only known from `Reply.lane` at the end. While a turn runs, "lane Task" means a
  `Phase::Tool` frame was seen. A task-lane turn that writes text before using any tool is
  queued, not steered. `alexia_core::lane::lane()` is still an empty stub on this branch.
- Phase words follow the spec's table. v2's status.js says "Asking a model…", "Trying again…" and
  "Asking another model…" for Asking, Retrying and Backup.
- An ack that answers after its turn was recorded is written in its own second `record_spans`.
