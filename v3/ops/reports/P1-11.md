What I built:
- `gateway.rs`: poll task per channel (ack spawned first), actor per chat (merge, restart, steer,
  queue, /stop /new /help), draft pump, deliver, spans, Say, shutdown. The brain runs in its own
  task so a cancel lets the v2 bridge stop itself (3 s, then abort). tokio `Instant` throughout.
- 16 integration tests (fake channel, brain, media, in-memory store) and 2 unit tests.

Fixes in this round:
1. Spans: every `start` is now ms from the turn's first receipt (`SpanRow.at` holds the receipt
   time): ack, merge (0), hear, brain, speak, deliver and command replies. `spans_cover_the_turn`
   asserts the relative contract (merge at 0, brain after the window, deliver after brain).
2. Brain spans are always placed at (brain hand-off offset) + `s.start`; the guess is gone.
3. Accepted as is: an ack that answers after its turn was recorded is written by a second
   `record_spans` call.
4. Accepted as is: "lane Task" while running = a `Phase::Tool` frame was seen. A task turn that
   writes text before any tool is queued, not steered.
5. Leak: messages dropped without a recorded turn (steer, stop, restart-cancel, a failed session
   lookup, the stop-cleared window and queue) have their `acks` entry removed; a reaction that
   answers after its message is gone is ignored (tracked in a `gone` set, cleared when it
   answers, so nothing grows). Unit test `acks_are_dropped_after_a_steer_and_a_stop` checks
   `acks`, `gone` and `late` are empty after a steer and after a stop.
6. A steer after a `Restart` sends nothing when the draft is empty (no bare "…"); test
   `steer_after_restart_sends_no_bare_ellipsis`.
7. Phase words stay as the spec table says (Choosing a model…, Thinking…, Working…, Trying
   another model…), not v2 status.js.
