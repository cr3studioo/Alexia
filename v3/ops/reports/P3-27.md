What I built:
- extract.js: hand-written HTML to main text (scripts, styles, nav, footer, forms removed; 20 000 cap).
- feeds.js: RSS and Atom parser, new-items-only, interest filter with diacritics folded.
- fetch.js: per-host queue with a 10 s gap, plain User-Agent, robots.txt, plain-sentence errors.
- portal.js: form login, in-memory cookies per visit, same-site only; a refused login is remembered for a day.
- watch.js: change detection, feeds, portal pages, notice items, brief section, status.
- index.js: tools page_text, notice_items, brief_section, check_now; interval timer; portal reset on settings change.
- plugin.json, README.md.
Fix round (reviewer), each with a test:
- SSRF: loopback, link-local, private (RFC 1918, CGNAT, fc00::/7) and reserved addresses refused after DNS resolution (IPv4-mapped, NAT64, 6to4 included), unless the owner listed that exact URL in pages or feeds; the portal's own origin (portal_url) counts as listed. Applies to every request.
- Redirects: followed by hand (max 5); address, robots.txt and the 10 s host gap are checked on every hop. request() never follows (always manual).
- Body: read as a stream and cancelled at 2 MB, so a huge page is never held in memory.
- Portal: an http portal_url or http login form action is refused before a password is sent.
- Noise: short lines that are only a clock, date or counter are dropped before hashing; lines with other words ("Mon 9:00 Math") stay. Notice key uses the full sha1. Old stored hashes are re-derived from stored text, so no one-off notice after upgrade.
