# P3-10 report

Status: done

What I built:
- `follow_up` builtin in `ScheduleTools` (remind | check_in | errand | timer). Each becomes a one-shot `Trigger::At` job in the calling chat, with the prompt stored as `follow:<kind>:<json>`. Undo is `schedule.cancel {id}`. Annotations are readOnly/destructive/openWorld false.
- Arguments: `at` is local "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM" (or T), read in the scheduler's zone. `in_minutes` must be above 0. A past time, or a time equal to now, is refused with a sentence, and so are bad kind/what/urgency values and `at` given together with `in_minutes`.
- `fire` hook: remind and timer file a notice (source `follow`, key `follow:<job id>`) and nudge the notice loop, with no model turn. A timer is filed Urgent when High would be held. check_in runs a turn ("Check in with the owner about: … Ask one short question.") and is said to the job's origin. An errand runs at 02:00 tonight by default; its reply is filed as a Low `errand` notice, so it waits for the morning brief.
- `schedule.list` shows follow-ups in words ("reminder: <what>"). `schedule.add` refuses a prompt that starts with `follow:`.
- `prompt.rs`: the FOLLOW_UP paragraph goes after the floor, before the owner's standing lines, only when the `follow_up` tool is available.
