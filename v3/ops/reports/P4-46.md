# P4-46: Gateway: the current session and moving it, for the window

Status: done

What I built:
- `Gateway::current_session(within_ms)` and `Gateway::moved_to(session, origin)` on P4-32's `Routes`; moves go through `Actor::move_to`. Each table entry now also carries its actor's session.
- Needed for them to be callable: `Gateway` is `Clone` and shares `Routes`, so a handle kept before `run(self)` works while it runs.
- `Origin::Window` is the face ("window", "main"): a registered "window" channel if there is one, else a face that shows nothing (the reply stays in the session). Turns and `Delivered` there carry `Origin::Window`.
- Unprompted `Say`: a configured channel named by `follow()` wins; else the "window" channel, if registered and the owner moved there within 30 min; else as before.

Problems or doubts:
- DECIDE: I asked the coordinator how a Say should "go to the window" (msg_b745ef62ad23) and got no answer; I took my recommended option. No "window" channel exists today, so in production a Say after a move to the window still goes to the chats. The test uses a fake one. P4-41 must register one (its paths: chat.rs only) or accept this.
- The spec says "the two methods only"; Clone, the per-entry session, the window face and the `followed_face` branch were needed besides them.
- P4-41: the hub must keep `gateway.clone()` before `run` and give it to the API; `main.rs` and `Api` are outside P4-41's listed paths.
- Without a "window" channel an in-flight reply is only in the session history: the window must reload on `Event::Delivered`; no live draft.
- `moved_to` does nothing before `run` or after cancel; call it inside the tokio runtime. It does not check that the session exists.
- `current_session` can be `None` for an instant after a chat's very first message, until its actor has read it.
- As noted in P4-32: a turn moved mid-flight keeps its start origin for approvals.
