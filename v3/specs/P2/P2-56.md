# P2-56: Scheduler: a cut-short one-shot survives shutdown; Every never overlaps itself

Model: opus
Depends on: none
Feature IDs: AC-09; review follow-up from P2-30

## Goal
Two faults in `alexia-scheduler`'s `run` loop.

A one-shot (`At`) job has its `next_at` set to None when it starts. `fire` cancels the job when
the turn ends, however it ended. At hub shutdown the in-flight turn ends `Cancelled`, so the
one-shot is dropped and never runs. After this task a one-shot whose turn was cut short by
shutdown fires again at the next start. It fires once, as a time that passed while the hub was
off does.

An `Every` job whose turn takes longer than its period is started again while the first turn is
still running. After this task a job never has two turns at once. A due time that arrives while
the job is still running is skipped and logged.

## Touch only these paths
- `v3/crates/alexia-scheduler/src/lib.rs` (change)

## Must not touch
Every other file, including `alexia-store` (`job_set_next` already takes any `Option<Ms>`).

## Interfaces
None new.

## Steps
1. `fire` (about line 271): on `Err(BrainError::Cancelled)` for an `At` job, do not
   `job_cancel`. Put `next_at` back to the job's `at` with `job_set_next(id, Some(at))`, so the
   next `run` after a restart sees it as overdue and fires it once. Record the run as `"stopped"`
   as today. A job cancelled by the owner (`schedule.cancel`) while running stays cancelled:
   check that `job_cancel` wins. Read the job row before you restore it and restore only when it
   is not cancelled.
2. `run`/`start` (about line 215): keep the set of job ids with a turn in flight, for example a
   `HashSet<i64>` next to `firing`, filled in `start` and emptied when the task ends (spawn
   returns the id, `join_next` removes it). `start` skips a job that is in the set: it still
   moves `next_at` on, logs `tracing::info!` once with the job id, and starts no turn.
3. Keep the comment about one-shots staying listed until their turn ends; update it for step 1.

## Tests
- `one_shot_cut_short_at_shutdown_fires_after_restart`: a brain that waits until cancelled, an
  `At` job due now, cancel `run`, then start a new `run` on the same store. The brain is asked
  again exactly once.
- `one_shot_cancelled_by_owner_while_running_stays_cancelled`
- `every_does_not_overlap_itself`: `Every` 50 ms, a brain that takes 300 ms. At most one turn is
  in flight at any time, and the job still fires again after the slow turn ends.

Each test must fail without its fix. Use the fake clock or `with_parts` hooks that
`tests/scheduler.rs` already has.
