# P3-09: Scheduler: morning brief, evening wrap-up, weekly review, while you were away

Model: opus
Depends on: P3-08
Feature IDs: PR-02, PR-03, PR-04, PR-22, PR-26 (the report of what she did silently), DY-16 (through `brief.section`)

## Goal
Every morning, at the first sign of the owner (or at the fallback time), she sends one brief:
the sections plugins offer (the day's plan, calendar, mail, health), the notices kept for the
brief, deadlines on the radar, and what she did silently since the last brief. In the evening a
wrap-up, on Sunday a weekly review, and after a long absence a short "while you were away".
Each is composed by code (PHASE.md D-P3-4), so it always arrives.

- `v3/crates/alexia-scheduler/src/lib.rs`   (change: start the brief clock, `brief_now`)
- `v3/crates/alexia-scheduler/tests/brief.rs`   (create)

## Interfaces
```rust
impl Scheduler {
    /// Compose and say a brief of `kind` now ("morning" | "evening" | "weekly" | "away").
    pub async fn brief_now(&self, kind: &str) -> Result<()>;
}
```
Builtin tool added to `ScheduleTools` (so core's `/brief` command and the agent can reach it
by tool name, without core depending on the scheduler): `brief_now {kind?: "morning" |
"evening" | "weekly"}` (default morning), read-only from the agent's point of view (it only
says things to the owner), result "Sent." or the error sentence.
```rust
// brief.rs
pub(crate) struct BriefParts { pub sections: Vec<String>, pub notices: Vec<NoticeRow>,
    pub radar: Vec<NoticeRow>, pub silent: Vec<AuditRow>, pub since: Ms }
/// Plain text, at most 3500 characters (one Telegram message; longer parts are cut with
/// "…and N more" lines, never mid-line).
pub(crate) fn compose(kind: &str, parts: &BriefParts, now_local: chrono::NaiveDateTime) -> String;
```
When each one is due (kv `_core`/`brief.last` = `{"morning": "YYYY-MM-DD", "evening": ..,
"weekly": "YYYY-Www", "away": Ms}`, local dates):
- **morning**: not yet said today, and either an `Event::Active` arrives at or after
  `brief_earliest`, or the clock reaches `brief_fallback`. Never inside quiet hours: an Active
  at 05:30 with quiet until 07:00 waits for 07:00. (PR-02's phone unlock arrives as an `Active
  { via: "phone" }` once something sends it, D-P3-7; nothing here changes then.)
- **evening**: at `cfg.evening`, not yet said today, skipped when quiet hours have started.
- **weekly**: at `cfg.weekly`, not yet said this ISO week.
- **asked**: kv `_core`/`brief.ask` = `{kind, at}` (written by `alexia-hub brief --now`,
  P3-31) with `at` newer than the last brief → `brief_now(kind)` at the next minute, then the
  key is deleted.
- **away**: an `Active` after at least 4 h with no `Active`, outside the morning window,
  when there are notices said and not seen, or held ones, since the last `Active`. At most
  once per 4 h.

Contents (`compose`), in this order, sections with no lines left out:
1. One opening line: "Good morning." / "Evening wrap-up." / "Your week." / "While you were away:".
2. `sources.sections(kind, now)`, each as given.
3. Radar: live notices with `due_at` in the next 7 days (morning, weekly) or tomorrow (evening),
   soonest first, as "• <text> (<due as weekday + date>)".
4. Notices `decide` put on `Brief` (state `new`), and for "away" those said-not-seen and held.
5. "Done on my own since <time>:" and one line per `audit_scheduled_since(since)` row, the
   tool's words (the audit's `why` or tool name), only when there is at least one (PR-26).
6. Evening only: "Anything I should remember from today?" (it is the journal question,
   answered in the chat like any message).

After saying it: every notice listed in 3 and 4 is `notice_said(id, "brief", now)`, and
`brief.last` is updated. The `Say` carries `notice: None`, `urgency: Normal`, `to: None`.

## Steps
1. `compose` as a pure function, tested on fixed parts.
2. The clock: a task in `run` that wakes at the next of the three times, on `Active`, and every
   minute, and calls `brief_now(kind)` when one is due.
3. `brief_now` gathers the parts and says it, whatever the time (for the CLI and `/brief`).

## Tests
`tests/brief.rs` (paused time, fixed tz, fake Sources, store in memory):
- `morning_at_first_activity`: Active at 06:40 (earliest 05:00, quiet ends 06:00) → a brief at
  06:40; a second Active at 06:50 → nothing more that day.
- `morning_waits_for_quiet_end`: Active at 05:30, quiet until 07:00 → brief at 07:00.
- `morning_fallback`: no Active → brief at 09:00.
- `brief_ask_key_is_honoured_and_cleared`.
- `one_morning_per_day_across_restart`: brief said, scheduler rebuilt → no second brief.
- `evening_and_weekly_times`, `weekly_once_per_iso_week`.
- `away_after_four_hours_with_news`, `away_not_without_news`.
- `compose_orders_and_cuts`: long parts are cut to 3500 chars with "…and N more".
- `silent_actions_listed`: two scheduler-origin audit rows appear under "Done on my own".
- `listed_notices_marked_said`.
