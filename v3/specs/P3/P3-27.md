# P3-27: Page watch: website changes, school portal, your interests

Model: sonnet
Depends on: P3-03
Feature IDs: PR-16, DY-11, PR-25

## Goal
The `page-watch` plugin watches pages the owner cares about and says what changed: a public
page (a course page, a timetable), the owner's school portal (signed in with credentials kept
in Credential Manager, read only), and topics they follow (RSS/Atom feeds and pages they
named), with new items summarised in the brief. It provides `page.text` so the scheduler's
`Trigger::Watch` can watch any page (P3-07), and `notice.items` for changes it finds itself.

## Touch only these paths
- `plugins/page-watch/**`   (fill: `index.js`, `fetch.js`, `extract.js`, `feeds.js`, `portal.js`, `plugin.json`, `README.md`, `test/*.test.js`)

## Must not touch
`package.json` (no new dependency: hand-written HTML-to-text and feed parsing), other plugins,
crates.

## Interfaces
- `requires`: `net.request` ("to read the pages you asked me to watch").
- Settings: `pages` (text, one URL per line), `feeds` (text, one URL per line), `interests`
  (text, topics, one per line), `portal_url` (text), `portal_user` (text), `portal_password`
  (password), `portal_pages` (text: paths to read after signing in), `every` (choice 30 | 60 |
  180 minutes, default 60).
- `page.text {url}` capability (read-only): the page's main text (scripts, styles, nav and
  footers removed, whitespace folded), at most 20 000 chars.
- Change detection: per URL, kv the last text's hash and the text (at most 20 000 chars);
  a change → a notice item with a diff summary (lines added/removed, at most 10 lines), key
  `pagewatch:<sha1 of url>:<hash>` (Normal for pages and portal, Low for feeds).
- Portal (`portal.js`): a form login (`POST` of user and password to the form found at
  `portal_url`, cookies kept in memory only), then read `portal_pages` as pages. A failed login
  is one status sentence and one Low notice a day, never a retry loop. Read only: it never
  submits anything but the login form. Owner needs 5 names the portal; if it uses a JS-only
  login, the README says this needs the browser limb (V3-4).
- Feeds: new items since the last read; `interests` filter by keyword (both languages,
  diacritics folded); `brief.section` morning: "From what you follow:" up to 5 items;
  weekly: up to 10.
- Politeness: at most one request per host per 10 s, a plain User-Agent
  "Alexia page watch (personal use)", honour `robots.txt` disallow for the watched path.

## Steps
`extract.js` and `feeds.js` pure with fixtures (hand-written HTML and feeds); `fetch.js` with
the rate limit and robots check over an injected fetch; `portal.js` over an injected fetch;
then tools and the timer.
