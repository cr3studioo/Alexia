# P3-32: A simulated week, proven

Model: sonnet
Depends on: P3-31, P3-14, P3-25
Feature IDs: PR-01, PR-02, PR-08, PR-09, PR-07, PR-26 (the phase's done-when)

## Goal
One test runs the hub's real wiring through seven simulated days and proves the phase's
done-when as far as code can: a morning brief every day at the right moment, a deadline
flagged while it was still `Low` or `Normal`, a promise raised and closed when the owner says
it is done, all unprompted, nothing said in quiet hours except what is urgent, and no tool run
on her own that the silent rule did not allow.

## Touch only these paths
- `v3/crates/alexia-hub/tests/week.rs`   (create)

## Must not touch
Anything under `src/` of any crate. If the test finds a bug, report it through the
coordinator with the failing assertion; the fix is a separate task.

## Interfaces
Uses only public APIs: `Store`, `Bus`, `Agent` with the scripted provider from
`tests/common/mod.rs` (as `agent_e2e.rs` uses it), `Scheduler::with_sources`, the gateway with
its fake channel, and a test `Sources` implementation that serves:
- `notice.items` from a fake "commitments" list (keys `fake:c1`…) and a fake calendar event
  with a deadline on day 4 at 17:00;
- `brief.section` "Today: <day name>";
- `notice.done` recording the keys it receives.
Time: `tokio::time::pause()` with a clock closure over a shared `Ms` the test advances,
through the scheduler's test constructor `with_parts` (P2-30; P3-07 gives it a `sources`
argument); a fixed time zone (UTC+1, no DST in the simulated week).
Owner activity: `Event::Active { via: "telegram" }` at 07:30 on weekdays, none on day 6
(Saturday) and 10:15 on day 7.

## Steps
1. Build the parts (store in memory, bus, scripted agent, fake channel gateway, scheduler with
   the test sources), quiet 23:00–07:00 letting `Urgent` through, brief earliest 05:00,
   fallback 09:00.
2. Day 1 to day 7: advance the clock in 5-minute steps, publish the scripted Actives, collect
   every `Say` and every fake-channel send with its time.
3. Day 2, 14:00: the owner's message "I'll send the form to Jana by Thursday" through the
   gateway; the scripted model answers by calling a fake builtin tool `commit` that the test adds
   to the tooling (`McpTooling::add_builtin`), and the fake source then serves it with
   `dueAt` Thursday 17:00.
4. Thursday 07:30: the brief lists it; the owner replies "done" to the notice message; the test
   checks `notice.done` received `fake:c2`, and the notice is `done`.

## Tests
`week::seven_days`, with these assertions (each its own `assert!` with a message saying which
done-when line failed):
- exactly 7 morning briefs; weekdays at 07:30; Saturday at 09:00 (fallback); Sunday at 10:15;
  none between 23:00 and 07:00;
- the day-4 deadline appears in a `Say` on day 1 or 2 while its notice's urgency is `low` or
  `normal`, before `radar` would make it `high` (**deadline flagged before it was urgent**);
- the promise from day 2 appears in Thursday's brief and is closed by the owner's "done"
  (**a promise followed through**);
- no `Say` has an owner message (gateway `Received`) in the 60 s before it, except replies
  (**unprompted**);
- a scripted proactive turn that calls a non-read-only fixture tool produced an Ask and did not
  run (**act silently only on the list**); after adding the tool id to kv `_core`/`silent` the
  same turn ran without asking; an `openWorldHint` tool still asked;
- an `Urgent` notice filed at 02:00 is said at 02:00; a `Normal` one filed at 02:00 is said at
  07:00.
