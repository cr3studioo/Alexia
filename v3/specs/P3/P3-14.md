# P3-14: Memory: life graph, people, goals, contacts, people nudges

Model: sonnet
Depends on: P3-13
Feature IDs: ME-01, ME-09, ME-16, DY-17, PR-19, PR-09 (dates from conversation)

## Goal
Memory grows a life graph: people (with how the owner knows them, birthdays, last time they
came up), dated things (deadlines, exams, appointments said in conversation), and goals the
owner is working towards, linked to the facts they came from. She can answer "what do you know
about Petr" from a people profile. Contacts from Google or Microsoft (`contacts.find`, when a
plugin provides it) fill in birthdays and addresses. The plugin offers `notice.items`, so the
scheduler hears about birthdays coming up, people the owner meant to get back to, deadlines
said in passing, and goals not touched in a while.

## Interfaces
New tables in the memory namespace (`storage.tables` += `people`, `dates`, `goals`):
- `people`: `name`, `aliases` (JSON array), `relation` ("sister", "supervisor"…),
  `birthday` ("MM-DD" or "YYYY-MM-DD"), `notes_ids` (JSON array of fact rowids), `last_seen`
  (ms, last time they came up), `follow_up` (text or null: "said he'd call back").
- `dates`: `what`, `due` ("YYYY-MM-DD" or "YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM"), `kind` ("deadline" | "event" |
  "anniversary"), `fact_id`, `state` ("open" | "past" | "done").
- `goals`: `text`, `since` (ms), `last_touched` (ms), `state` ("active" | "done" | "dropped").

Tools (annotations as `remember`'s: not read-only, not destructive, not open-world, except
the read-only ones):
- `about_person {name}` → the profile: relation, birthday, the facts, the last time, the
  follow-up. Read-only.
- `people {}` → one line per person. Read-only.
- `goal {text}` / `goal_done {id}` / `goals {}`.
- `notice_items {now, horizon_days, tz_offset_min}`, `_meta: {"alexia/provides": ["notice.items"]}`,
  read-only, returns the §3.6 shape (`structuredContent.items`), keys prefixed `memory:`:
  - birthdays within 7 days (urgency Low, `due_at` that day 09:00 local);
  - open `dates` within `horizon_days` with `due_at` (urgency left to the scheduler's radar);
  - people with a `follow_up` older than 3 days (PR-19, Low, why "You said you'd get back to
    them.");
  - active goals untouched for 14 days (Low, once per 14 days per goal).
- Contacts (DY-17): when `contacts.find` has a provider, `about_person` and the nightly pass
  (P3-15) look the person up and fill `birthday` and a contact line. Without it, nothing
  changes and nothing is said.

Capture: the sorting prompt also returns `people: [{name, relation?, birthday?, follow_up?}]`,
`dates: [{what, due, kind}]` and `goals: [{text}]` for the facts it writes, each linked to its
fact id. Only things the owner said about their own life. A date with no year is the next
such date. A relative date ("next Friday") is resolved by the plugin from the message's own
`at`, not left to the model.
