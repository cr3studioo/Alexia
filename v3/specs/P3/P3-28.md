# P3-28: Health nudges

Model: sonnet
Depends on: P3-02
Feature IDs: PR-20

## Goal
The apple-context plugin, which already imports selected Apple Health data, offers gentle
nudges to the proactivity engine: two short nights in a row, no workout in several days, a
resting heart rate well above the owner's usual. Each is a `Low` notice for the next brief,
never an alarm, worded as an observation, and the owner can switch each kind off. Its README's
"No proactive messages" line changes to say exactly what it now offers.

## Touch only these paths
- `plugins/apple-context/nudges.js`   (create)
- `plugins/apple-context/index.js`   (change: `notice_items`, `brief.section`)
- `plugins/apple-context/plugin.json`   (change: settings, provides)
- `plugins/apple-context/test/nudges.test.js`   (create)
- `plugins/apple-context/README.md`   (change: the proactive line)

## Must not touch
The importer and schema (`store.js`, `schema.js`, `transport.js`), the Shortcuts assets,
other plugins, crates.

## Interfaces
- Settings: `nudge_sleep` (toggle, on), `nudge_activity` (toggle, on), `nudge_heart` (toggle,
  off), `sleep_hours` (number, 6), `rest_days` (number, 5).
- `notice_items` (`notice.items`, read-only), keys `health:<kind>:<date>`, urgency Low:
  - sleep under `sleep_hours` on each of the last two nights → "Two short nights: 5 h 10 and
    5 h 40." why "From your sleep data."
  - no workout in `rest_days` days → "No workout since Tuesday." why "From your workouts."
  - resting heart rate's 3-day mean ≥ 10 bpm above its 30-day median → "Resting heart rate is
    higher than usual this week." why "Compared with your last 30 days." Off by default.
  - Nothing when the data is missing or older than 2 days (say nothing rather than guess).
- `brief.section` weekly: one line per kind with the week's numbers, only kinds switched on.
- `notice.done` is not offered: these are observations, closed by the scheduler when they
  vanish.

## Notes for the worker
- No Rust. Cargo checks, if run: `CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=1`.
- Health data is sensitive: numbers go only into the notice text the owner reads, never into a
  log. Tests use synthetic records, as the plugin's existing tests do.
- Not medical advice: wording is an observation, never a diagnosis or a recommendation to
  treat anything.
- Invariants: no plugin names in crates, no user paths, no secrets.
