# P3-29: Study desk: study mode, meeting and lecture notes

Model: sonnet
Depends on: P3-03
Feature IDs: DY-12, DY-18

## Goal
The `study-desk` plugin turns a lecture or meeting recording into notes (transcript through
`voice.transcribe`, then a summary with key points, terms and open questions, saved as
Markdown in a folder the owner chose), and runs study sessions: questions drawn from those
notes and from documents in the library, spaced so that what the owner got wrong comes back
sooner. Exam dates feed the scheduler: reviews are offered in the days before.

## Touch only these paths
- `plugins/study-desk/**`   (fill: `index.js`, `notes.js`, `cards.js`, `plugin.json`, `README.md`, `test/*.test.js`)

## Must not touch
`package.json` (no new dependency), the voice plugin (use `voice.transcribe`), other plugins,
crates.

## Interfaces
- `requires`: `voice.transcribe` ("to turn a recording into words"), `fs.read_scoped` and
  `fs.write_scoped` ("to read the recording and save the notes in the folder you chose").
- Settings: `notes_folder` (folder setting type; inside chosen roots).
- Tables: `notes` (`title`, `path`, `course`, `at`), `cards` (`note_id`, `q`, `a`, `box` 1..5,
  `due` "YYYY-MM-DD"), `exams` (`course`, `day`).
- Tools: `lecture_notes {path, title?, course?}` (not read-only, not destructive: writes a new
  Markdown file, never overwrites: a name clash gets " (2)"), `study {course?, minutes?}`
  (read-only: returns the next cards to ask, the brain asks them one by one), `answer_card {id,
  right}` (Leitner: right → box+1, due in 1/2/4/8/16 days; wrong → box 1, due tomorrow),
  `exam {course, day}`, `exams {}`.
- Notes: one sampling call per 20 minutes of transcript chunk, then one to merge; the file
  has a header (title, date, course), "Key points", "Terms", "Open questions", then the
  transcript under a fold (`<details>`).
- `notice_items`: cards due today (Low, one item "12 cards to review"), exams within 14 days →
  item with `dueAt` (radar raises), key `study:exam:<id>`.
- `notice.done`: `study:exam:<id>` closes the exam.

## Steps

tools with fake capabilities.
