# P3-26: Tasks, and reminders by place or person

Model: sonnet
Depends on: P3-03
Feature IDs: DY-07, DY-08 (place, person), DY-10 (where quick captures land)

## Goal
The `task-list` plugin keeps the owner's to-dos and lists (tasks, shopping, anything they
name), with due days, and reminders tied to a place ("when I'm at the gym") or a person ("next
time I talk to Petr"). It is where quick captures land (`tasks.add`, P3-31's `/note` and "+"
messages). Due tasks reach the scheduler through `notice.items`. Place reminders fire from
apple-context's place events (`context.current`), and person reminders fire when that person
comes up in conversation (memory's people, `memory.recall`).

## Touch only these paths
- `plugins/task-list/**`   (fill: `index.js`, `lists.js`, `triggers.js`, `plugin.json`, `README.md`, `test/*.test.js`)

## Must not touch
`package.json` (no new dependency), apple-context and memory (use them by capability), crates.

## Interfaces
- `requires`: none hard. Optional: `context.current` ("to know when you arrive somewhere you
  named"), `memory.recall` ("to know who a reminder is about").
- Tables: `items` (`list` default "tasks", `text`, `due` "YYYY-MM-DD" or null, `state` open |
  done, `place` or null, `person` or null, `at`, `done_at`).
- Tools: `tasks.add` capability (`{text, due?, list?}` → `{id}`, not read-only, not
  destructive: on the proposed silent list), `add_task {text, due?, list?, place?, person?}`
  (same, with triggers), `done_task {id}`, `tasks {list?, state?}` (read-only), `lists {}`
  (read-only). Never a delete tool: done is the end (the proposed silent list says "never
  delete").
- `notice_items`: open items with `due` (dueAt that day 18:00 local, radar raises), keys
  `tasks:<id>`; place items whose place matches `context.current`'s latest place event within
  the last 15 minutes (Normal, why "You're at <place>."); person items whose person was named
  in a turn in the last 10 minutes (Normal, why "You're talking about <person>."): the plugin
  learns that from `notice.items`' `now` and a `memory.recall` for the person's name
  restricted to the last 10 minutes, if the memory plugin supports a `since` argument;
  otherwise person reminders fire in the next morning brief after the person was mentioned
  (state this limit in the README).
- `notice_done {key}` (`notice.done`): closes `tasks:<id>`.
- `brief.section`: morning: today's due and overdue; weekly: the week's due ones and the
  shopping list's length.
