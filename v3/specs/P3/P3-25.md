# P3-25: Commitments: keeps your promises, early

Model: sonnet
Depends on: P3-02
Feature IDs: PR-08, PR-09 (promises with a day), ME-02 (promises caught in conversation)

## Goal
The commitments plugin (v2) becomes a source for the proactivity engine: it offers
`notice.items`, so a promise the owner made with a day is raised while there is still time
(the scheduler's radar decides how loudly), on the day itself, and after it if still open.
Promises without a day are raised once a week until closed. When the owner answers "done" to
one of those notices, the commitment closes. This is the "at least one promise followed
through" of the phase's done-when.

## Interfaces
- `notice_items` (`_meta alexia/provides ["notice.items"]`, read-only), INTERFACES §3.6 shape,
  keys `commitments:<rowid>`:
  - open with `by` within `horizon_days` or overdue → `dueAt` = `by` at 17:00 local (from the
    caller's `tz_offset_min`), urgency omitted (the radar raises it), text = `line(row)`, why
    "You said you would." or "You were asked to." (the `mine` field: reminded vs nagged).
  - open without `by`, last raised more than 7 days ago (column `raised_at`) → Low, why "Still
    open, no day set."
  - The plugin records `raised_at` = now for every item it returns. That is not a write the
    owner sees, and the scheduler dedupes anyway.
- `brief.section`: `weekly` → "Promises: N open, M overdue" and the overdue lines. Other kinds
  empty (the notices carry the daily ones).
- `notice_done {key}` (`_meta alexia/provides ["notice.done"]`, not read-only, not
  destructive): when `key` is `commitments:<rowid>` and that commitment is open, close it as
  `kept` and answer `{closed: true}`; any other key → `{closed: false}`, nothing changed.
  The scheduler fans `notice.done` out to every provider when the owner answers **Done** to a
  notice (P3-08 through `Sources::done`), and each provider ignores keys that are not its own.
  So no crate ever names this plugin.
