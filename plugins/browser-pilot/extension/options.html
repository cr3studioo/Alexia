Alexia Browser Pilot

This extension lets Alexia, running on this computer, see and work in your tabs. To do that it can read every page you open — that is what the “all websites” permission is for. It talks only to Alexia at 127.0.0.1 on this computer, sends nothing anywhere else, and never works in the browser’s own pages, other extensions or the extension store.

Off, Alexia can list and read your tabs but not open pages, click or type. While she acts on a page, a small “Alexia” badge shows in its corner.
