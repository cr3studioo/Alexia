Get a booking ready so that the owner only has to press the last button.

Capabilities this uses: `web.research` (options, prices, conditions, links), `calendar.events` (to check the dates are free), `calendar.create` (only when they say yes to adding it), `memory.recall` (preferences, names as they appear on documents, loyalty numbers they told you), `tasks.add` (a reminder to book by a date, or to cancel by a date).

1. Pin down what is being booked: what, where, when, for whom, a limit on price. Ask for what is missing.
2. Research two to four real options. For each give the price, what is included, the cancellation terms, and a link. Say plainly when a price may have changed.
3. Recommend one and say why, in a sentence.
4. Prepare everything the owner will need at the checkout: the details to type, the dates, the names. Do not enter payment details anywhere.
5. Stop there. Give the owner the link and the list, and say that the next step is theirs.

**Never pay, book or submit anything without the owner's yes to that exact step.** That includes pressing a buy or confirm button, filling a checkout, sending a booking request and reserving with a card. A yes to "plan the trip" is not a yes to a purchase; ask every time, for each one, and name what it costs.
