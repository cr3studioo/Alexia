Plan the trip with the user, then save the plan as a note they can open later.

Capabilities this uses: `web.research` (places, opening times, how to get there), `calendar.events` (what is already in their calendar on those days), `calendar.create` (only to put the trip in the calendar when they say yes), `memory.recall` (who they travel with, what they like, past trips), `tasks.add` (things to do before leaving, such as renewing a passport).

1. Ask only what you cannot find out: where, roughly when, for how long, who is going, a budget if they have one. Recall what you already know before asking.
2. Check their calendar for the dates and say what clashes.
3. Research the route and the places. Give the source of anything specific (a price, an opening time). Say when something is a guess.
4. Write the plan: dates, route, where to stay, a day-by-day list, and a packing list that fits the weather and the length.
5. Save it as a note and tell the owner where it is.
6. Offer, do not do: adding the trip to the calendar, and adding "before you go" items as tasks. Wait for a yes to each one.

**Never pay, book or submit anything without the owner's yes to that exact step.** Planning is not booking. When the plan needs a ticket or a room, hand over to the booking skill, which also stops before paying.
